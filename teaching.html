Teaching

Applied technology, taught under pressure.

Courses across AI, UI/UX, enterprise systems, emergency management, and geospatial science — built on practical work, ethical framing, and the habits people need when tools change faster than institutions.

The teaching begins with use. Students and faculty need enough conceptual structure to understand what a system is doing, enough fluency to work with it, and enough skepticism to know when the output needs review. That balance runs through every course.

The paramedic background shows up in the shape of the work — explanation to scenario, scenario to decision, decision to consequence. The classroom is calm; the habits are built for rooms that aren't.
