The AI Human

The book frames AI adoption as a promotion. Human value shifts from execution to stewardship, and that shift asks educators, leaders, and students to build new habits for agency, ethics, judgment, and review.

It focuses on the work that survives software updates: scoping tasks, evaluating outputs, protecting critical thinking, and designing human-centered routines for automated systems. The intended audience includes educators building AI-aware courses, leaders guiding institutional adoption, and students preparing for a workplace where judgment matters more because automation is everywhere.
