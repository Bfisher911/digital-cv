The AI Human

AI adoption is a promotion. Human value shifts from execution to stewardship — and that shift asks educators, leaders, and students to build new habits for agency, ethics, judgment, and review.

The book focuses on the work that survives software updates: scoping tasks, evaluating outputs, protecting critical thinking, and designing human-centered routines for automated systems. It's written for educators building AI-aware courses, leaders guiding institutional adoption, and students preparing for a workplace where judgment matters more because automation is everywhere.
