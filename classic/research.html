MissDelta

MissDelta, the Mississippi River Delta Transition Initiative, is a $22M, five-year National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine funded project. The consortium includes 14 institutions and focuses on the Birdsfoot Delta. Dr. Fisher contributes remote sensing and geospatial science work.
