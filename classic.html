Biographical record

About

Dr. Blaine Fisher works where technology has consequences. His professional record spans enterprise AI adoption, instructional technology, emergency management, geospatial science, consulting, and teaching. The common thread is practical judgment inside complex systems. He helps people use new tools without losing sight of ethics, context, or the humans who have to live with the result.

At Tulane University, he serves as Senior Instructional Technology Manager in the Innovative Learning Center. Since October 2012, he has worked across platform adoption, AI strategy, faculty development, and learning infrastructure for a 13,000-student institution. He administers Canvas across academic units, leads AI-focused faculty capability work, and founded WAVE, Tulane's AI Visionaries and Educators Network.

His faculty work extends across Tulane's School of Professional Advancement and the School of Science and Engineering. He teaches information technology, emergency management, GIS, and remote sensing, with courses that range from AI in Modern Society to Health and Medical Issues in Emergency Management. His research life moves into the field: the Birdsfoot Delta through MissDelta, and Classic Maya defensive landscapes at Dos Aguadas, Guatemala.

Dr. Fisher is the author of The AI Human: Navigating a Transformed World, the Pracademics Editor and Editorial Board Member at Homeland Security Today, and founder of Sparkfish Consulting, LLC. Before his academic technology career, he worked as a paramedic in the New Orleans region. That background still shows. He teaches and advises like someone who has seen plans meet weather, pressure, scarcity, and time.
